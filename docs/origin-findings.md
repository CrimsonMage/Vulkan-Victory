# What origin/main does that made its world lit, against our Vulkan path

Source: `/mnt/data/victory/scratch-agent/forks/origin-main` (fxceless/victory-fork `6742906`, the
D3D12 path, lit and playable on an NVIDIA card), its README ("Newest"/"Earlier" entries and the
summary), the 129 commits since our merge-base `0b0074e0b`, and the translator history before it.
"origin" paths are under its `crates/victory-shader/src/`; "ours" under our
`crates/victory-shader/src/vk/` unless a crate is named. Read on 2026-11-26/27; nothing here was run
on a card. Verdicts: SAME, DIFFERENT (both do something, not the same), MISSING (we lack it),
N/A (their fix is for a D3D12 construct we do not have), HOST (outside the translator; not
verified here).

The order is by how likely a row explains (a) the lighting buffer black after the tiled-lighting
draws, (b) the fog apply pass's huge R/A on sky pixels, (c) glyph boxes.

## Rows that could explain (a), (b), (c)

| 0 | **A load of several dwords is bounds-checked dword by dword.** "The console checks each dword on its own; a host may check a call of several dwords as a whole and answer ZEROS for all of them when only the last lies past the view's end (the lead for run 277's black water)." The case they name: four dwords at byte 328 of a **340-byte** constant view, of which the program uses three (a `float3` at the buffer's end): "90 lighting programs have that load". | `emit/lower.rs` `raw_dwords` (commit b1d777a): one call for up to four dwords only where all of them lie inside the view (`getDimensions`), else one call a dword. Their grouped loads without that guard (`VICTORY_SHADER_OPT=127`) blackened the water in run 277 and stayed off until the guard. | `lift_mem.rs` `access_addr` / `in_bounds`: `offset + 4n <= limit` for the **whole access** (`buffer_load_raw` for `s_buffer_load_dwordx2..x16` in groups of four, `buffer` for `buffer_load_dwordx2..x4`), else the address is the zero block: **all n dwords read 0** when only the last one passes the end. `docs/vulkan-abi.md` section 4 states it as the rule ("whole access, not per dword"). | **DIFFERENT: ours is the behaviour they found black** | **(a)**: the light and view constants a lighting program reads from the tail of a constant buffer are zeros (lights black); every stage. `s_buffer_load_dwordx4` 21,499 programs, `x8` 14,198, `x16` 14,389, `buffer_load_dwordx2..4` 12,974 / 5,281 / 11,720. |
| 1 | **Whole-wave sections.** Between `s_or_saveexec -1` and the restore of EXEC every lane of the wave runs, the inactive ones with an identity (`v_cndmask`), through a DPP tree, `v_permlanex16` and `v_readlane 0`: the compiler's `WaveActiveMin/Max/BitOr/Sum` and prefix sums. Hardware lanes always exist; a host lane that is elsewhere cannot run that code. | `emit/wave.rs` (`sections`, `simulate`, `Shape`) simulates the section symbolically; `emit/lower.rs` `find_sections`, `section_inst`, `shape` lower each value that leaves it as `waveActiveOp` / `waveActiveBit` / `wavePrefixOp` over the host's active lanes of `mask ? src : identity`. | `crate::wave` is in our tree but **unused** (lib.rs: "kept for their port into `vk/`"). The section is run lane by lane: `lift_lane.rs` `dpp` and `lift_vops.rs` `op_permlanex16_b32` through `lift_wave.rs` `shuffle_live` (a source lane that is not there gives the lane's own value), `v_readlane` through `readlane_live` (lane 0, or the first live lane). **That loses every relay through a lane that is not there**: with lanes 0-11 live, lane 0 ends with the minimum of lanes 0-7 only (rows 8-11 reach lane 0 only through lanes 12-15). Right only when all 32 invocations of the subgroup exist and take part in shuffles: true for our compute groups, not for pixel subgroups (partly filled; helper invocations may be left out of non-quad subgroup operations). | **MISSING** | **(a)**: every `TL_PS_*` program walks its tile's lights from three such sections (minimum and maximum mask word, OR of the word's bits; `dis_tl.txt` 5616-5936), then loops over the bits. A wrong minimum/maximum/OR is "no lights" or the wrong ones for the whole subgroup. 2,285 programs use `v_permlanex16_b32`, 3,042 `v_readlane_b32`. Also removes most of the 1,281 pixel programs' "approximations" (reductions across wave64 halves). |
| 2 | **Ray/node tests while no structure is built.** The title's ray-traced passes (sun and lamp shadows, bounce light) run even when nothing builds their structures; each node test then reads whatever bytes lie there, differently from launch to launch. | `emit/intersect.rs` `no_hit()`: while the builder is off a box node enters no child (4 × `0xffffffff`), any other node is a miss (`bvh::MISS`): nothing is read, shadow rays find no occluder. (`VICTORY_BVH_NO_HIT`, default on with the builder off; test `a_node_test_that_answers_no_hit_reads_nothing`.) | `lift_image.rs` `bvh` + `emit_bvh.rs` always read the node at `(T# base << 8) + (pointer >> 3) * 64` from title memory, although the host leaves the builder's dispatches out unless `VICTORY_BVH_BUILDER=1` (`victory-gpu/src/vulkan/record.rs` `prepare_dispatch`). | **DIFFERENT** | **(a)** through the shadow masks and the ray-traced light the tiled lighting reads: with garbage nodes a shadow ray can hit (light black) or walk the title's 65,535-step bound. 64 programs. |
| 3 | **Pixel quad reads see helper lanes.** `ds_swizzle_b32` in quad mode and DPP `quad_perm` read the other pixels of the lane's 2×2 quad (hand-made derivatives); at a triangle's edge those are helper lanes. Wave reads leave helper lanes out. | `emit/lower.rs` `quad_read` (after Belobug 03629f3f): `quadOp` for swaps across x, y, the diagonal, `quadReadLaneAt` otherwise, in pixel and mesh programs. Test `a_pixel_quads_lanes_are_read_through_quad_reads` (`emit/mod.rs`). Their symptom before: "triangle edges showed as lines on car paint, glass and skin", "square holes and dashed edges in shadows". | `lift_mem.rs` `ds_cross` and `lift_lane.rs` `dpp`/`quad_perm`: `OpGroupNonUniformShuffle` from `(SubgroupLocalInvocationId & !3) \| sel`, and "an inactive source lane reads 0". This assumes that lanes 4k..4k+3 of a host subgroup are one pixel quad in the console's order and that helper invocations answer shuffles; Vulkan promises neither (only `OpGroupNonUniformQuadSwap/Broadcast` are quad-aware). `s_wqm` (`lift_scalar.rs` `wqm`) makes the same assumption with a ballot. | **DIFFERENT** | derivatives taken by hand: mip choice of `image_sample_d` (2,880 programs), edges of every surface; 6,270 programs use `ds_swizzle_b32`. Not the whole-buffer black of (a) by itself. |
| 4 | **A helper lane's first-lane read is its own value.** In whole-quad mode helper lanes are in EXEC, so a waterfall loop (read the first lane's value, run the lanes that hold it, drop them from EXEC) serves them too. | `emit/lower.rs` `helper_reads_own` (after upstream 0601955): `isHelperLane ? own : waveReadLaneFirst`. Before it: such loops ran to the loop limit in every helper lane (draws of 0.3-1.3 s, then DEVICE_HUNG). Test `a_pixel_first_lane_read_gives_a_helper_lane_its_own_value`. | `lift_valu.rs` `valu_lane0`: `BroadcastFirst`, or a shuffle from the first bit of the ballot of EXEC; with no bit in the ballot (only helper lanes left and the host leaving them out of ballots) it reads lane 31. Our blocks branch on `OpGroupNonUniformAny`, so such a loop ends when the live lanes are done, but what the helper lanes computed is then undefined. | **MISSING** | TL_PS has 25 `v_readfirstlane_b32`; 16,392 programs use it. Loop-guard hits (the guard buffer's count) in pixel programs would be this. |
| 5 | **Reads of registers nothing wrote read 0** (SGPRs as VGPRs). | `ir.rs`/`emit/lower.rs` (upstream f4438b3 "reads of uninitialized SGPRs read as zero, as VGPRs do"). | `lift_io.rs` `register_input`: an unwritten VGPR is `Op::Undef` → `OpUndef` (`emit.rs:337`); phis of a missing path are `Op::Undef` too (`lift_flow.rs:55,279,345`). | 6 | **NaN and infinity tests must survive the host compiler.** With fast-math flags the driver folded the title's own NaN tests away: "black blotches on skin and clothes". | `emit/lower.rs` `is_nan_f32` (NaN tests on the bits), `VICTORY_SHADER_FAST_BITS` 24 (no nnan/ninf), compare lowering at "Every comparison that a NaN makes true". Their test is not ported yet (task 1). | SAME in intent, **unproven** | **(b)**: `inf * 0` in `v_mul_legacy_f32` (99 of them in CS_ApplyCombinedDeferred). |
| 7 | **`v_mul_legacy_f32`: a zero factor gives +0** whatever the other is; **`v_mad_legacy_f32` is fused and flushes denormal sources** on RDNA 2 (it decodes as `v_fma_legacy_f32`). "The brightest water reflections no longer have black cores." | `emit/lower.rs` legacy multiply; "A legacy multiply-add as RDNA 2 runs it: fused, with the legacy rule" (pull request #1026). | 8 | **Implicit-level samples outside pixel shaders.** The console takes the derivatives from neighbouring lanes (compute programs run `s_wqm`); 730 compute programs sample this way, "among them the G-buffer resolves and the sky". | `emit/lower.rs` (image lowering, "Outside a pixel shader there are no screen derivatives"): level 0; `image_sample_b` there: the bias **is** the level.
